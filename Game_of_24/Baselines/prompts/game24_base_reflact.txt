# Task Description
Use numbers and basic arithmetic operations (+ - * /) to obtain 24.

# Rules
1. Before making your choice, reflect on the current situation: state which numbers are still left, and relate that state to the goal of reaching 24 (for example, which factor of 24 you are trying to build, or which pair can combine toward 24). A reflection grounds where you actually are before you act, so it must be based on the latest "numbers left" observation, not on a number you wish you had.
2. Choose two numbers from the remaining ones and apply one of these operations: addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (*), or division (/).
3. When observation shows that there is only one number, 24, you should give a complete expression for that number that uses only the four numbers in the input. Such as "answer: (6 - 4) * (4 + 8) = 24".

# Here are 5 examples.

## Example 1
Input: 4 4 6 8
Act 1> reflection: I still have all four numbers 4, 4, 6, 8 and nothing is combined yet; my goal is to reach 24. Since 24 = 2 * 12, and 4 + 8 = 12 while 6 - 4 = 2, I can build both factors. I will make 12 first.
Obs 1> OK.
Act 2> 4 + 8 = 12
Obs 2> numbers left: 4 6 12
Act 3> reflection: The numbers left are 4, 6, 12. To reach 24 I need to turn 4 and 6 into the factor 2, because 2 * 12 = 24. Subtracting 6 - 4 gives that 2.
Obs 3> OK.
Act 4> 6 - 4 = 2
Obs 4> numbers left: 2 12
Act 5> reflection: The numbers left are 2 and 12, and 2 * 12 = 24 lands exactly on the goal, so I will multiply them.
Obs 5> OK.
Act 6> 2 * 12 = 24
Obs 6> numbers left: 24
Act 7> reflection: Only 24 remains, so the goal is reached; the complete expression using all four input numbers is (6 - 4) * (4 + 8) = 24.
Obs 7> OK.
Act 8> answer: (6 - 4) * (4 + 8) = 24
Obs 8> Success!

## Example 2
Input: 2 9 10 12
Act 1> reflection: I still have all four numbers 2, 9, 10, 12; my goal is to reach 24. I notice 12 * 2 = 24 directly, which leaves 9 and 10 that I must reduce to a neutral 1, since 24 * 1 = 24. I will make 24 from 12 and 2 first.
Obs 1> OK.
Act 2> 12 * 2 = 24
Obs 2> numbers left: 9 10 24
Act 3> reflection: The numbers left are 9, 10, 24. To keep the 24 unchanged I need a 1 from 9 and 10, and 10 - 9 = 1. I will compute that.
Obs 3> OK.
Act 4> 10 - 9 = 1
Obs 4> numbers left: 1 24
Act 5> reflection: The numbers left are 1 and 24, and 24 * 1 = 24 preserves the goal value, so I will multiply them.
Obs 5> OK.
Act 6> 24 * 1 = 24
Obs 6> numbers left: 24
Act 7> reflection: Only 24 remains, so the goal is reached; the complete expression is (12 * 2) * (10 - 9) = 24.
Obs 7> OK.
Act 8> answer: (12 * 2) * (10 - 9) = 24
Obs 8> Success!

## Example 3

Act 2> 13 - 10 = 3
Obs 2> numbers left: 3 4 9

Obs 3> OK.

Obs 4> numbers left: 4 6

Obs 5> OK.

Obs 8> Success!

## Example 5
